D10 INSTALLATION RECOVERY · NEBULAR 437Q-N

Recover your tag

Restore the backup saved before your interrupted D10 installation. Keep the tag powered and paused until recovery finishes. Your files stay on this computer.

1. Choose your saved files

Choose the failed installation report and the complete backup saved before installation. The report tells this page which tag and backup to expect. Keep a separate copy of the backup.

Use “Save report” on the failed installer, or choose a text file containing its complete JSON report.

No backup selected.

2. Connect the programmer

If there is a wire between GP2 and the tag’s RST pad, remove it. Leave the SWS connection, power and ground connected. Keep power on. Do not press reset or BOOTSEL.

Programmer GP2← remove this wire →Tag RST

Only SWS is used for data. You do not need the tag’s UART TX or RX pads connected. Disconnecting GP2/RST prevents an accidental reset when the browser opens the connection. Your programmer is already v1.6; it does not need a firmware update.

Close other flasher or serial-monitor connections first. Click the two buttons in order. For the second, choose the port labelled SWS (the first interface), rather than Tag UART.

Choose your report and backup, then confirm GP2 is disconnected from RST.

3. Check, then restore

The check reads your tag twice, confirms its identity and checks that its factory data matches your backup. It keeps the tag paused. After it passes, click “Restore saved backup”. The restore repeats these checks before writing.

Complete the steps above to begin.

Leave this page open and the tag powered until recovery finishes. The final restart happens only after the firmware and protection checks pass.
