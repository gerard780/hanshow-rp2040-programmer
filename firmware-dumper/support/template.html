Recover your tag

This page is set up for your interrupted installation. It restores your original firmware and can install D9 in the same session. You do not need Python, commands or a report file.

1. Choose your original backup

Choose the complete .bin backup saved before the failed installation. If the old page still offers an emergency recovery backup, save that file first. Keep the tag powered.

No backup selected.

2. Choose what happens next

Checking the included D9 firmware…

3. Connect the programmer

If there is a wire between GP2 and the tag’s RST pad, remove it. Leave the SWS connection, power and ground connected. Keep power on. Do not press reset or BOOTSEL.

Programmer GP2← remove this wire →Tag RST

Only SWS is used for data. You do not need the tag’s UART TX or RX pads connected. Disconnecting GP2/RST prevents an accidental reset when the browser opens the connection. Your programmer is already v1.6; it does not need a firmware update.

Close other flasher or serial-monitor connections first. Click the two buttons in order. For the second, choose the port labelled SWS (the first interface), rather than Tag UART.

Choose your backup and confirm GP2 is disconnected from RST.

4. Check, then recover

The check reads your tag twice and confirms it matches this recovery. It does not erase or program firmware. After it passes, click the green button.

Complete the steps above to begin.

Leave this page open and the tag powered until recovery finishes. The final restart happens only after the firmware and protection checks pass.
